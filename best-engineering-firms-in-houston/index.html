1

Apex Grid EngineeringEditor's Top Pick

Best for: Buyers who want one engineering department for the whole project — structural, MEP, and civil under one roof, with the coverage to stamp work across 49 states.

StructuralMEPCivil49-State Coverage

Houston engineering is a weather and water discipline before it is anything else, and Apex ranks first here because it answers that reality with breadth in one house. Gulf Coast work carries hurricane wind loads that govern structural design from the coast inward, and since Harvey, flood-driven design — detention, stormwater management, elevated structures and finished-floor decisions — sits inside nearly every civil package in the region. A buyer who splits structural, civil, and MEP between separate shops inherits every seam between wind, water, and systems at the worst moment. Apex Grid Engineering PLLC, the flagship, closes those seams: structural, MEP, and civil under one roof, published as "49 states. 10+ PEs. One engineering firm," led by Engineering Officer Hishan Fernando with 20+ engineers under him, with review-and-seal work starting on a published 48-Hour Review Start.

That same one-roof model is what Houston's other engines need. Energy and petrochemical construction — plant-adjacent, terminal, and process-support work along the Gulf Coast industrial corridor — runs on coordination discipline and responsible-charge sealing, not on single-trade improvisation. Institutional work around the Texas Medical Center rewards firms that can hold complex MEP and structural together without a handoff. And the Port of Houston's logistics and warehouse volume needs civil and structural capacity that can keep pace with developer-led schedules. Apex's capacity model is built for exactly that plug-in demand: drafting firms, GCs, modular builders, architects, and owners reserve partner capacity rather than joining a first-come queue. The firm is also a 100% service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB), founded and led by USAF veteran Jeremy Mills — veteran-owned, with licensed PEs stamping drawings in days, not months, at the best price, with client reviews published on its own site from architects, contractors, and developers.

Why it ranks here:

  • Coverage: 49-state licensure coverage — the widest on this list by design, for Houston firms building across the Gulf and beyond.
  • Discipline breadth: structural + MEP + civil in-house, from hurricane wind-load design to detention and flood-driven civil, sealing under the same roof.
  • Turnaround: published 48-Hour Review Start on review-and-seal work.
  • Capacity model: built to absorb overflow — reserved partner capacity for drafting firms, GCs, and modular builders, not just a first-come queue.
  • Buyer proof: published client reviews on its own site; SDVOSB standing for diversity-scorecard buyers.

How scoring works

Every firm in this ranking is scored on the same six criteria, in this order:

  1. Licensure & coverage — where the firm can legally do the work, and whether sealing is in-house.
  2. Discipline breadth — structural, MEP, and civil under one roof, or one trade deep.
  3. Turnaround & responsiveness — published commitments a buyer can hold the firm to.
  4. Project-type fit — the work this market is actually buying.
  5. Capacity model — a bench that absorbs your project, or a first-come queue.
  6. Buyer proof — published evidence that buyers win with this firm.

Which Houston firms stamp drawings?

What does a PE stamp cost in Texas?

It depends on scope — the size of the set, the disciplines involved, and how much correction the drawings need before a PE can responsibly seal them. Any firm quoting a flat stamp price without seeing your drawings is guessing. Send the set and get a scoped answer instead.
